# Privacy Policy — Radio

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Radio ("the app") is an internet radio player for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't collect, store or share any personal information.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves these on your phone only. They are never sent to us:

- your favorite stations
- the last station you played
- your theme choice (light / dark / system)

Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Network connections

To work, the app connects to:

- **Radio Browser** (`api.radio-browser.info`), a free public directory, to fetch the list of stations and to search. When you play a station, the app tells Radio Browser it was played so popular stations rank higher. No personal data is included.
- **The radio station's own streaming server** when you play it.
- **Station logo addresses** provided by the station directory.

Like any internet connection, these servers can see your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** to stream radio.
- **Notifications:** to show the player controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
- **Foreground service (media playback):** to keep playing when the app is in the background or the screen is off.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
